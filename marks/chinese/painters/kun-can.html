    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Kun Can in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.

      Beijing Municipal Administration of Cultural Heritage 北京市文物局 · ink and color on paper · inscribed “癸卯（康熙二年，1663）冬病中⋯•識於牛頭山，

      按：此圖乃細筆山水。鑑定初期，諸老偶有戲謔之

      • Xu Bangda 徐邦達: “I saw it twenty years ago; it's already recorded in 'Gu shuhua guoyan yaolu'.” 二十年前我已看過，已收錄到《古書畫過眼要録》去了。

      • Xu Bangda 徐邦達: “He was often ill. 'Youqi' is the temple and 'Jieyun' the room — a room in the temple.” 他經常生病的。“幽棲”是寺，“借雲”是房，寺裹的房。
      • Xie Zhiliu 謝稚柳: “It is the same as Shitao's 'Yizhi ge' (Single Branch Pavilion); Fu Baoshi made it into a single branch of bamboo, and that made a joke of it — a joke is fine, but this was too big a joke. Cheng Qingxi was very close to him; the inscription mentions it.” 等於石濤的“一枝閣”，傅抱石把他搞成一枝竹就鬧笑話了，鬧笑話可以，但鬧得太大了。程青溪與他甚好，題衰談到。 Translator’s note: 題衰 read as 題裏 'in the inscription'
      • Xu Bangda 徐邦達: “He was not clean; he lived in seclusion and rarely came out; only when Cheng Qingxi wanted him to go out did he take a bath.” 他不講衛生，深居簡出，難得出來，也是程青溪要他出去才洗一次澡。
