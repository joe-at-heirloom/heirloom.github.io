    Name
    Dai Benxiao 戴本孝
    Dates
    1621–1694 or after 明天啟元年——清康熙三十三年後（公元1621—1694年後）
    Other names
    務旃 Wuzhan; 鷹阿 Yinga (Eagle hollow); 鷹阿山樵 Yinga shanqiao (Woodcutter of Eagle Hollow); 前休子 Qianxiu zi (Master of former rest); 破琴老生 Poqin lao sheng (Old scholar who smashed the zither)
    Native place
    安徽和縣 Anhui, Hexian
    Known for
    山水 landscape
    Role
    painter

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Dai Benxiao in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
