    鄭燮
    Name
    Zheng Xie 鄭燮
    Dates
    1693–1765 清康熙三十二年——乾隆三十年（公元1693—1765年）
    Other names
    克柔 Kerou; 板橋 Banqiao (Plank Bridge); 板橋道人 Banqiao daoren (Daoist of Plank Bridge); 風子 Feng zi (Wind master); 樗散人 Chu sanren (Idler of the useless tree); 紅雪山樵 Hongxue shanqiao (Woodcutter of Red Snow Mountain)
    Native place
    江蘇興化 Jiangsu, Xinghua
    Known for
    花卉 flowers, 蘭 orchids, 竹及書法, 篆刻 seal carving
    Writings
    Banqiao's inscriptions on paintings 板橋題畫
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Zheng Xie in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item.
