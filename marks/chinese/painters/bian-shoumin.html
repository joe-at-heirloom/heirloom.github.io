    Bian Shoumin 邊壽民

    邊壽民
    Name
    Bian Shoumin 邊壽民
    Dates
    清雍正——乾隆間（公元十八世紀）
    Other names
    原名維祺 Yuan ming Weiqi (Original name Weiqi); 頤公 Yigong; 漸僧 Jianseng (Gradual monk); 葦間居士 Weijian jushi (Lay Buddhist among the reeds); 綽綽老人 Chuochuo laoren (Old man of ease); 墨仙 Moxian (Ink immortal)
    Native place
    江蘇淮安 Jiangsu, Huaian
    Known for
    蘆雁, 花卉及山水
    Role
    painter

    The book gives a period, not years. See Wikidata Q835579.

    Seals and signatures

Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Bian Shoumin in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members.
