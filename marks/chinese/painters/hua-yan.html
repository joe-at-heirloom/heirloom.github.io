    In the Qing imperial catalogs

    The Qianlong emperor had his court catalog the painting and calligraphy in the palaces: 石渠寶笈 (secular works) and 祕殿珠林 (Buddhist and Daoist works), in 1744–45, with further series in 1793 and 1816. These 1 entries are recorded under Hua Yan’s name, with the hall where each was kept and, in the first series, the court’s grade. A listing means the emperor owned an item with that title; it does not show that any particular scroll is that item.
