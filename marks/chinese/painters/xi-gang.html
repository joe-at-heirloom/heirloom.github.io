    Xi Gang 奚岡

    奚岡
    Name
    Xi Gang 奚岡
    Dates
    1746–1803 清乾隆十一年——嘉慶八年（公元1746—1803年）
    Other names
    初名鋼 Chu ming Gang (Originally named Gang); 鐵生 Tiesheng; 純章 Chunzhang; 鶴渚生 Hezhu sheng (Scholar of Crane Isle); 蒙道士 Meng daoshi (Daoist Meng); 蝶野子 Dieye zi (Master of the butterfly wilds); 散木居士 Sanmu jushi (Lay Buddhist of useless timber); 蒙泉外史 Mengquan waishi (Unofficial historian of the Meng Spring); 蘿龕外史 Luokan waishi (Unofficial historian of the Vine Shrine); 冬心先生 Dongxin xiansheng (Master of the Winter Heart); 冬花庵主 Donghua an zhu (Master of the Winter Flower Hermitage)
    Native place
    浙江杭州 Zhejiang, Hangzhou
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 花卉及書法, 篆刻 seal carving
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Xi Gang in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
