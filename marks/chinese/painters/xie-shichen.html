    Seals and signatures

    The 37 seals and signatures the Shanghai Museum’s book records for Xie Shichen, numbered as in the book (entry 1134, from p. 1530), each with its reading, the work it is on, and that work’s date where known. Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Xie Shichen in the first volume (collections in Beijing), with the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item.
