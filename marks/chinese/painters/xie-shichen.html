    Xie Shichen 謝時臣

    謝時臣
    Name
    Xie Shichen 謝時臣
    Dates
    1487–1567 or after 明成化二十三年——隆慶元年後（公元1487—1567年後）
    Other names
    樗仙 Chuxian (Useless-tree immortal); 思忠 Sizhong; 樗仙子 Chuxian zi (Master of the useless tree); 虎丘山人 Huqiu shanren (Mountain man of Tiger Hill)
    Native place
    江蘇蘇州 Jiangsu, Suzhou
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 人物 figures, 花卉 flowers
    Role
    painter

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Xie Shichen in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
