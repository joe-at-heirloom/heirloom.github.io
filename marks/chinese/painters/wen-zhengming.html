    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Wen Zhengming in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. These 211 entries are recorded under Wen Zhengming’s name, with the hall where each was kept and, in the first series, the court’s grade. A listing means the emperor owned an item with that title; it does not show that any particular scroll is that item.
