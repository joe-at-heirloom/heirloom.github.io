    Wen Zhengming 文徵明

    文徵明
    Name
    Wen Zhengming 文徵明
    Dates
    1470–1559 明成化六年——嘉靖三十八年（公元1470—1559年）
    Other names
    初名璧 Chu ming Bi (Originally named Bi); 一作壁 Yi zuo Bi (Also written Bi); 徵仲 Zhizhong; 衡山 Hengshan (Mount Heng); 衡山居士 Hengshan jushi (Lay Buddhist of Mount Heng); 停雲生 Tingyun sheng (Scholar of Halted Clouds)
    Native place
    江蘇蘇州 Jiangsu, Suzhou
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 花卉 flowers, 蘭竹, 楷書 regular script, 行書 running script
    Writings
    Wen Daizhao's colophons 文待詔題跋
    Role
    painter, calligrapher
