    Gong Xian 龔賢

    龔賢
    Name
    Gong Xian 龔賢
    Dates
    1618–1689 明萬曆四十六年——清康熙二十八年（公元1618—1689年）
    Other names
    一名豈賢 Yi ming Qixian (Also named Qixian); 半千 Banqian (Half a thousand); 野遺 Yeyi (Wild bequest); 半畝 Banmu (Half acre); 柴僧 Chaiseng (Brushwood monk); 柴丈人 Chai zhangren (Elder of the brushwood); 野遺生 Yeyi sheng (Scholar of wild bequest); 半山野人 Banshan yeren (Rustic of Half Mountain); 掃落葉僧 Sao luoye seng (Monk who sweeps fallen leaves); 鍾山野老 Zhongshan yelao (Old rustic of Mount Zhong)
    Native place
    江蘇崑山 Jiangsu, Kunshan
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 行書 running script
    Writings
    Secrets of painting; Manual of painting methods 畫訣、畫法冊
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Gong Xian in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
