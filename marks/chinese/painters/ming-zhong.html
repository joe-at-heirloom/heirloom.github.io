    Ming Zhong 明中

    明中
    Name
    Ming Zhong 明中
    Dates
    清雍正——乾隆間（公元十八世紀）
    Other names
    俗姓施 Su xing Shi (Secular surname Shi); 演中 Yanzhong; 大恆 Daheng; 㝧虛 Yuxu (Lodging in emptiness); 紫衣僧 Ziyi seng (Monk in purple robes); 中道人 Zhong daoren (Daoist Zhong); 北山主人 Beishan zhuren (Master of North Mountain); 白雲峯主 Baiyun feng zhu (Master of White Cloud Peak); 慧日峯主 Huiri feng zhu (Master of Wisdom Sun Peak); 牧牛行者 Muniu xingzhe (Pilgrim who herds the ox)
    Native place
    浙江桐鄉 Zhejiang, Tongxiang
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 花卉 flowers, 寫照及篆刻
    Role
    painter, seal carver

    No matching entry was found in Wikidata, so the dates rest on the book alone.

    Seals and signatures

    The 26 seals and signatures the Shanghai Museum’s book records for Ming Zhong, numbered as in the book (entry 395, from p. 574), each with its reading, the work it is on, and that work’s date where known. Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Ming Zhong in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
