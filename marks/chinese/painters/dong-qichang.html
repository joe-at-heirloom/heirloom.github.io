    董其昌
    Name
    Dong Qichang 董其昌
    Dates
    1555–1636 明嘉靖三十四年——崇禎九年（公元1555—1636年）
    Other names
    玄宰 Xuanzai (Dark steward); 思白 Sibai (Thinking white); 香光居士 Xiangguang jushi (Lay Buddhist of fragrant light); 思翁 Siweng (Thinking old man)
    Native place
    上海松江 Shanghai, Songjiang
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 楷, 行, 草書 cursive script
    Writings
    Random notes from the Painting Chan Studio; Aims of painting; The eye for painting 畫禪室隨筆、畫旨、畫眼
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990
