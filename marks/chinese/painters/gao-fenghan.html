    高鳳翰
    Name
    Gao Fenghan 高鳳翰
    Dates
    active 1683 清康熙二十二年——乾隆十三年（公元1683年—1748年）
    Other names
    仲威 Zhongwei; 西園 Xiyuan (West garden); 南村 Nancun (South village); 南阜 Nanfu (South mound); 老阜 Laofu (Old mound); 櫱琴翁 Nieqin weng (Old man of the sprout zither); 息園叟 Xiyuan sou (Old man of the Rest Garden); 尚左生 Shangzuo sheng (Scholar who honors the left); 南阜老人 Nanfu laoren (Old man of the South Mound); 雲阜山人 Yunfu shanren (Mountain man of Yunfu); 丁巳殘人 Dingsi canren (Survivor of the dingsi year); 天祿外史 Tianlu waishi (Unofficial historian of Tianlu)
    Native place
    山東膠縣 Shandong, Jiaoxian
    Known for
    山水 landscape, 花卉及書法, 篆刻 seal carving
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

    The book gives a period, not years. See Wikidata Q890282.

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Gao Fenghan in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
