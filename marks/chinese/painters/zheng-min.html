    Seals and signatures

    The 65 seals and signatures the Shanghai Museum’s book records for Zheng Min, numbered as in the book (entry 1074, from p. 1448), each with its reading, the work it is on, and that work’s date where known. Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    Let Heirloom read the marks for you

    Photograph the seals and the signature. The Advisor reads them and names the people they claim, with the record each comes from; it never says whether a work is genuine.
