    Seals and signatures

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Zha Shibiao in the first volume (collections in Beijing), with the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item.
