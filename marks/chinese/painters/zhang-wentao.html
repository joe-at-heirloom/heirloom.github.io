    Zhang Wentao 張問陶

    張問陶
    Name
    Zhang Wentao 張問陶
    Dates
    1764–1814 清乾隆二十九年——嘉慶十九年（公元1764—1814年）
    Other names
    仲冶 Zhongye; 樂祖 Lezu (Delighting in ancestors); 船山 Chuanshan (Boat mountain); 老船 Laochuan (Old boat); 豸冠僊史 Zhiguan xianshi (Immortal historian in the xie-beast cap); 蜀山老猿 Shushan lao yuan (Old ape of Mount Shu); 藥庵退守 Yaoan tuishou (Retired guardian of the Herb Hermitage); 寶蓮亭主 Baolian ting zhu (Master of the Precious Lotus Pavilion); 羣僊之不欲昇天者 Qun xian zhi bu yu sheng tian zhe (Immortal who did not wish to ascend to heaven)
    Native place
    四川遂寧 Sichuan, Suining
    Known for
    書法及山水, 花鳥 birds and flowers
    Role
    painter, calligrapher

    Checked against Wikidata Q11067741: the years agree.

    Seals and signatures

Seals come first and signatures after. A seal a person stamped on someone else’s work, as an owner, is marked as a collector’s seal.

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Zhang Wentao in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item.
