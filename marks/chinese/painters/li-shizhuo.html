    Li Shizhuo 李世倬

    李世倬
    Name
    Li Shizhuo 李世倬
    Dates
    1687–1765 or after 清康熙二十六年——乾隆三十年後（公元1687—1765年後）
    Other names
    漢章 Hanzhang; 星垞 Xingcha; 毅齋 Yizhai (Studio of resolve); 蒞園 Liyuan (Garden of attendance); 天濤 Tiantao (Heavenly waves); 清在居士 Qingzai jushi (Lay Buddhist of pure presence); 丁卯客 Dingmao ke (Guest of the dingmao year); 伊祁山人 Yiqi shanren (Mountain man of Yiqi); 十石居士 Shishi jushi (Lay Buddhist of ten stones); 太平拙吏 Taiping zhuo li (Clumsy official of Taiping); 芥道人 Jie daoren (Mustard seed Daoist); 敬原主人 Jingyuan zhuren (Master of the Reverent Source)
    Native place
    遼寧瀋陽 Liaoning, Shenyang
    Known for
    繪畫
    Role
    painter

    In the national survey, 1983 to 1990

    From 1983 to 1990 a group of China’s leading connoisseurs examined the painting and calligraphy in the country’s public collections; their secretary, Lao Jixiong, recorded each work and what was said about it. These are the works by Li Shizhuo in Lao’s record, with the collection that held each, the group’s verdict and the remarks of named members. The verdicts are theirs; Heirloom repeats them and does not judge any item. Titles and remarks are translated by Heirloom, with the original Chinese beside each; where the source text is unclear, a translator’s note says so.
