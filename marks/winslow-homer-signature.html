Winslow Homer's signature, 1862 to 1909

14 close-ups of Winslow Homer's signature (American, 1836–1910), from works dated 1862–1909, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

Homer's Civil War field drawings of 1862 are signed simply 'Homer' with the year, in graphite.

How Winslow Homer signed

  • Through the 1870s and 1880s his watercolors are most often signed 'HOMER' in capitals with the year, sometimes shortened to an apostrophe and two digits, as in 'HOMER '89'.
  • On The Red School House (1873) a signature 'Homer 1873' was scratched into the wet paint and then painted over; the visible signature reads 'HOMER / 1873'.
  • Yachting Girl (1880) carries a copyright notice in his hand: 'Copyright - 1880 Winslow Homer'.
  • Homer sometimes added a dedication years after a work was made: Hauling in the Nets, signed 'Winslow Homer 87', is also inscribed 'To Mr M.F. King. with compliments of the Artist Sept 30th 1893'.
  • Danger, a watercolor begun in 1883, is signed twice: 'Winslow Homer / 1883' and 'Winslow Homer 87'.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Winslow Homer sign paintings?

Homer's Civil War field drawings of 1862 are signed simply 'Homer' with the year, in graphite.

Did Winslow Homer always sign the same way?

No. The 14 examples on this page, from 1862–1909, show copyright notice with full name, full name and date, full name and short date, full name in capitals and date. Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Winslow Homer?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Winslow Homer is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
