Vincent van Gogh's signature, 1879 to 1890

11 close-ups of Vincent van Gogh's signature (Dutch, 1853–1890), from works dated 1879–1890, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

Van Gogh signed only 133 of his 840 known paintings, about 15 per cent.

How Vincent van Gogh signed

  • He almost always signed with his first name only; in March 1888 he wrote that his name should appear in catalogues as he signed it on the canvases, "Vincent and not Vangogh", because people in France could not pronounce it.
  • He signed no paintings before 1884, although he had started painting in oils in 1881; he signed drawings earlier.
  • He signed more often in Paris and soon after arriving in Arles; after entering the asylum at Saint-Rémy in 1889 he signed hardly any paintings, and in Auvers he signed only one, a portrait he gave to its sitter, Adeline Ravoux.
  • He often signed paintings he gave to family and friends, and usually left unsigned the copies he made after prints by Millet, Rembrandt and Delacroix.
  • On Seascape near Les Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer (1888) he wrote that he put "a very outrageous red signature" in the lower left "because I wanted a red note in the green".

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Vincent van Gogh sign paintings?

Van Gogh signed only 133 of his 840 known paintings, about 15 per cent.

Did Vincent van Gogh always sign the same way?

No. The 11 examples on this page, from 1879–1890, show date only, in the plate (no signature), first name, first name and date with dedication, first name with place and date. Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Vincent van Gogh?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Vincent van Gogh is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
