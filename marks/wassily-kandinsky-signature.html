Wassily Kandinsky's signature, 1901 to 1928

13 close-ups of Wassily Kandinsky's signature (Russian, later German and French, 1866–1944), from works dated 1901–1928, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

Kandinsky's early Munich paintings are signed 'KANDINSKY' in capital letters; on Schwabing, Winter Sun (1901) it is in red at lower right, with title, place and date written on the back.

How Wassily Kandinsky signed

  • By the 1920s he signed paintings on the front with a monogram the Pompidou in Paris reads as 'VK' and a two-digit year, as on Auf Weiss II ('VK 23', lower left).
  • On the back of later paintings he added the monogram, a running number from his own handlist and the year, as on Ein Kreis (A): 'VK EIN KREIS a, N°420/1928'.
  • The 1922 Bauhaus portfolio Kleine Welten carries the 'VK 22' monogram in the printing surface and a pencil signature 'Kandinsky' on each sheet.
  • To Nina for Christmas (1926) is monogrammed 'VK 26' on the front and dedicated on the back in Russian to his wife Nina, dated Christmas Eve 1926, Dessau.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Wassily Kandinsky sign paintings?

Kandinsky's early Munich paintings are signed 'KANDINSKY' in capital letters; on Schwabing, Winter Sun (1901) it is in red at lower right, with title, place and date written on the back.

Did Wassily Kandinsky always sign the same way?

No. The 13 examples on this page, from 1901–1928, show monogram and two-digit date, monogram and two-digit date, in the stone, monogram cut in the block, surname (pencil). Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Wassily Kandinsky?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Wassily Kandinsky is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
