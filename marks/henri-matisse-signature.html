Henri Matisse's signature, 1899 to 1929

12 close-ups of Henri Matisse's signature (French, 1869–1954), from works dated 1899–1929, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

On the 1913 lithograph Nude in a Rocking Chair, Matisse's initials 'H.M' are drawn in the stone and printed, and each sheet is also signed 'Henri - Matisse' and numbered by hand in pen.

How Henri Matisse signed

  • His 1920s lithographs, such as the 1927 Dix danseuses portfolio, are signed 'Henri - Matisse' in pencil together with the edition number (for example '97/130').
  • On the 1923 etching Femme au collier the signature 'Henri Matisse' is etched into the plate and prints mirror-reversed.
  • The 1909 painting Nude with a White Scarf is signed with a two-digit date: 'Henri-Matisse 09'.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Henri Matisse sign paintings?

On the 1913 lithograph Nude in a Rocking Chair, Matisse's initials 'H.M' are drawn in the stone and printed, and each sheet is also signed 'Henri - Matisse' and numbered by hand in pen.

Did Henri Matisse always sign the same way?

No. The 12 examples on this page, from 1899–1929, show full name, full name (black chalk), full name and date, full name with edition number (pen). Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Henri Matisse?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Henri Matisse is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
