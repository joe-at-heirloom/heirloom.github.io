Frederic Remington's signature, 1889 to 1908

10 close-ups of Frederic Remington's signature (American, 1861–1909), from works dated 1889–1908, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

Remington signed his paintings and drawings with his full name, 'Frederic Remington', often in capitals with a long flourish before and after.

How Frederic Remington signed

  • Some paintings made for reproduction carry a copyright notice beside the signature, such as 'Copyright 1890 / By / Frederic Remington' on A Mexican Vaquero and 'Copyrighted / Frederic Remington / '89' on The Mexican Major.
  • He also signed and dated pen-and-ink illustrations, for example 'Frederic Remington 1889' on On the Bridle Path, Central Park.
  • His bronzes are signed in the cast, usually 'Frederic Remington' on the base, often with 'Copyright' or 'Copyrighted' and the founder's mark; on one cast of The Bronco Buster a '55' is worked into the F of the signature.
  • The Frederic Remington catalogue raisonné records each work's signature and inscriptions by position, and notes copyright only where a record from his lifetime was found.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Frederic Remington sign paintings?

Remington signed his paintings and drawings with his full name, 'Frederic Remington', often in capitals with a long flourish before and after.

Did Frederic Remington always sign the same way?

No. The 10 examples on this page, from 1889–1908, show copyright notice and full name in capitals, copyright notice, full name in capitals, short date, full name, full name and date. Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Frederic Remington?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Frederic Remington is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
