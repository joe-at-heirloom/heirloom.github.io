Amedeo Modigliani's signature, 1914 to 1919

14 close-ups of Amedeo Modigliani's signature (Italian, 1884–1920), from works dated 1914–1919, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

An early portrait, Jean-Baptiste Alexandre with a Crucifix (1909), is signed and dated "Modigliani 1909" at the upper left in dark blue.

How Amedeo Modigliani signed

  • Not every painting was signed: Paul Alexandre in Front of a Window (1913) is neither signed nor dated.
  • On late portraits such as Portrait of a Girl (about 1917) the signature "modigliani" is at the top right in slightly thinned dark paint, without a date.
  • On Self-Portrait as Pierrot (1915) he painted the word PIERROT on the picture as well as his name, and the back is inscribed "MODIGLIANI 1915".
  • Most of the National Gallery of Art's 1917–1919 portraits are signed "modigliani" in lower case at the upper right; Madame Kisling is signed at the lower right.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did Amedeo Modigliani sign paintings?

An early portrait, Jean-Baptiste Alexandre with a Crucifix (1909), is signed and dated "Modigliani 1909" at the upper left in dark blue.

Did Amedeo Modigliani always sign the same way?

No. The 14 examples on this page, from 1914–1919, show inscribed motto, signature and address, surname in capitals, surname in lower case, surname with capital M, in red. Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by Amedeo Modigliani?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by Amedeo Modigliani is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
