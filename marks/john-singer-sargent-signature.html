John Singer Sargent's signature, 1878 to 1916

13 close-ups of John Singer Sargent's signature (American, 1856–1925), from works dated 1878–1916, in date order. Each is cut from a museum photograph of the work and links to the museum's record.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

In 1878, the year he first showed at the Paris Salon, Sargent signed En route pour la pêche in capitals: 'JOHN S. SARGENT. / PARIS 1878'.

How John Singer Sargent signed

  • From about 1879 his usual signature in oil is a cursive 'John S. Sargent', often with the year and sometimes the place, as in 'John S. Sargent / Ronjoux 1879'.
  • Several of his full-length portraits split the signature and the date between the two upper corners, for example 'John S. Sargent' upper left and '1888' upper right on the portrait of Eleanora Iselin.
  • Sargent often gave portraits and sketches as gifts with a dedication: Miss Beatrice Townsend (1882) is inscribed 'to my friend Mrs Townsend / John S. Sargent', for the sitter's mother.
  • Many of his early sketchbook drawings carry a number such as 'J.S.238' added later; the National Gallery catalogues these as written by an unknown hand, not as his signature.

What a signature can't tell you

Signatures were copied, added to unsigned works and printed onto reproductions. A signature that looks like these is a lead, not an answer, and Heirloom never decides whether a work is genuine. For how to read a signature and where to look one up, see artist signatures.

Questions

How did John Singer Sargent sign paintings?

In 1878, the year he first showed at the Paris Salon, Sargent signed En route pour la pêche in capitals: 'JOHN S. SARGENT. / PARIS 1878'.

Did John Singer Sargent always sign the same way?

No. The 13 examples on this page, from 1878–1916, show full name, full name (date '1888' painted separately upper right), full name (date '1907' painted separately upper right), full name and date. Signatures change with age, medium and the size of the work.

Does a matching signature mean a work is by John Singer Sargent?

No. A signature is the easiest part of a work to copy or add. Whether a work is by John Singer Sargent is a question for a specialist in the artist's work and, where one exists, the catalogue raisonné.
