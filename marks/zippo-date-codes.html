Zippo date codes

The stamp on the bottom of a Zippo dates its case to the month. Type the code to read it.

By Heirloom · Updated November 22, 2026

In short

A Zippo's date code is stamped on the bottom of the case. Since July 1986 the letter on the left is the month (A is January, L is December) and the right side is the year: Roman numerals from II for 1986 to XVI for 2000, then two digits from 01 for 2001. K XII is November 1996; E 12 is May 2012.

Decode a Zippo date code

Type the letter on the left of the stamp and the numeral or number on the right. ZIPPO and BRADFORD, PA. can be left out.

How to read the code

The code is stamped on the bottom of the lighter's case, around the word ZIPPO. Since July 1986 it has two parts:

  • On the left, the month: a letter from A for January to L for December.
  • On the right, the year: a Roman numeral from 1986 to 2000, counted from II for 1986 to XVI for 2000; from 2001, the last two digits of the year (01 for 2001, 12 for 2012).

Before July 1986: dots and slashes

From the 1950s the bottom stamp carried a year code of dots or lines beside the words, with no month. Zippo's 2005 guide sets the patterns out as dots from 1958 to 1965, vertical lines from 1966 to 1973, forward slashes from 1974 to 1981 and backslashes from 1982 to the middle of 1986. Zippo Canada's list agrees but ends the backslashes in 1985, and Zippo's sources differ on whether the codes began in the mid-1950s or in 1958. Count the marks against Zippo's own chart; this page's lookup reads only the letter codes.

What the code can't tell you

  • It dates the case. The code is on the bottom of the case, and that is what it dates.
  • It doesn't prove a lighter is a Zippo. A stamp can be copied. Heirloom reads the code; it never decides whether an item is genuine.

The lookup above gives the same answer as Heirloom Advisor: it reads exactly one month letter and one year, and gives nothing when the code is incomplete or reads two ways.

Questions

How do I date a Zippo lighter?

Turn it over and read the bottom stamp. Since July 1986 the letter on the left is the month (A for January to L for December) and the right side is the year: a Roman numeral from II (1986) to XVI (2000), then two digits from 01 (2001).

What does K XII mean on a Zippo?

November 1996. K is the eleventh letter, November, and XII is the Roman numeral for 12, counted from II for 1986.

What do the dots or slashes on the bottom of a Zippo mean?

They are the year code used before July 1986: dots, then vertical lines, then forward and back slashes, each pattern standing for a year. Zippo's own chart lists them.

Does a date code mean my Zippo is genuine?

No. It tells you when the case says it was made. A code can be copied, and Heirloom never decides whether an item is genuine.
