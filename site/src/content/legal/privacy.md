# Heirloom Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026 · **Last updated:** October 30, 2026

The Peninsular Company, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Detroit ("Heirloom," "we," "us," or "our"), operates the Heirloom mobile and web application (the "App" or "Service"), which helps you keep an inventory of what is in your home: what each item is, its photos and its story, the documents and evidence behind its value, and who should inherit it. From that inventory it can prepare a document for your insurer or your family. Where you choose, it uses artificial intelligence to help identify and research your items, and, for accounts in the United States, it can connect you with independent specialist businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we
share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. Please read it
together with our Terms of Service.

By using Heirloom, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in
this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Who we are

Heirloom is provided by The Peninsular Company, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Detroit. For any
privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact us at **hello@heirloomapp.io**.

Heirloom is offered in the United States, to people who live there. Heirloom's iPhone App is offered on the App Store in the United States. The web App can be reached from other countries, and a relative abroad can open a collection shared with them; an account outside the United States is not offered specialists, sale conversations, sold prices or AI-estimated value ranges (Section 3.1).

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our
representative / Data Protection contact is hello@heirloomapp.io (we have not appointed a formal EEA/UK representative or Data Protection Officer).

Insurance policy documents you attach to a policy you record are uploaded and kept in private storage;

**Estate-planning information.** If you use our estate-planning features, you may provide

**If you would consider selling an item.** We keep that choice, the country and, if you give it, the state or region you chose for it, and your note (Section 6).

**If you ask to be told when an item like yours sells.** For each item where you turn this on, we keep that you did and when, and when we will next look. It is offered only to accounts in the United States, and you can turn it off on the item at any time.

**Other things you record.** Reports you file and the accounts you block, personal contacts you add to your directory (name, email, phone, company, address, website and role), and the insurance policies you record (provider, policy number, coverage, premium and renewal date).

**Payments.** We take no payment from people who keep an inventory in the App, and we collect no payment information. A specialist business that buys introduction credits is invoiced outside the App (Section 12.1).

## 4. How artificial intelligence features work

**AI identification and research are optional and need your permission.** The App asks
before it sends anything to an AI or search provider, and nothing is sent until you choose
Allow. You can record your items yourself instead, and you can withdraw permission at any
time in **Settings → Advisor consent**. Once you allow it, to help identify, describe, and
research your items, we send the relevant item photos and descriptive text to third-party
AI providers. Today we use **OpenAI** for image analysis, for reading marks and labels in
your photos, and for the in-app Advisor chat. For finding comparable sales, we submit a
temporary link to the item's photo to a reverse-image search service (SerpAPI, which
queries Google Lens) and text queries built from the item's details, such as maker, type
of item, period, and material, to a market-research search service (SerpAPI, which queries
Google Search). We may in the future use additional AI providers (for example Anthropic);
if we do, they will receive the same categories of item data for the same purpose, and we
will update this Policy.

**Sold prices.** To find sales of the same kind of item, we send the search service the maker, form and one distinguisher on the record (a pattern, model or shape number). Our server then reads the public sale pages that search finds, eBay's included, without signing in to any site, and sends those pages nothing of yours. It reads them under its own name, HeirloomBot, and does not read a page that the site's robots.txt asks it not to.

**A question about everything you own.** If you ask the Advisor about your whole inventory, we send OpenAI the name, type, maker and value of every record you own, and no photos.

**Items you share.** Something about an item goes to these providers only if the item's owner has allowed it. If someone you share a collection with asks the Advisor about one of your items, both of you must have allowed it, and a Viewer or a Commenter is answered only from what their share shows (Section 6).

**Please note:** AI-generated identifications and valuations are **automated, uncertain
estimates for your information only. They are not professional appraisals, authentications,
or insurance valuations**, and you should not rely on them for insurance, tax, legal,
estate, or sale decisions without independent professional advice.

**AI request logging.** For quality, debugging, and abuse-prevention purposes, our backend
logs each AI request. By default a log entry keeps what diagnosis needs — which feature
ran, its timing, status, model, and cost, and your account and item identifiers — and not
your descriptions, the AI's answers, or links to your photos. To investigate a specific
problem we may briefly turn on more detailed logging, which also records the text sent to
and returned by the AI provider and links to the photos involved. All AI request logs are
deleted automatically after a short period (currently about seven days).

You can turn the setting off at any time; a specialist who has already seen the card cannot un-see it, and the item shows how many have.

For each person or invitation link, you choose whether they also see any of five things: values (your estimates, appraisals and matched sales), serial and model numbers, documents, where the item is kept, and purchase details (what you paid, when you acquired it, and its provenance). An Editor or an Admin sees everything you see. Photos and words are shown as they are, so a photo of a serial plate, or a story that mentions a price, is seen by anyone who sees the item.

Before anyone signs in, an invitation link shows whoever opens it your first name, the collection's name, how many items it holds and the role offered, and nothing else. A messaging app that draws a preview under the link shows your first name, the collection's name and the number of items.

What a person you invited writes (a memory, a suggested correction, a comment) can be read by you and by the collection's current members, and is deleted if that person deletes their account. If they leave the collection or are removed from it, what they wrote stays with the collection and they no longer see it, and an item they added to your collection stays in the collection.

Recording a beneficiary or a bequest does not grant access to anyone, and we do not tell the people you name. Passing an item's record to another person is not offered in the current version of the App; a record someone accepted from you earlier is theirs (Section 9).

**With identity providers.** Apple and Google when you use their sign-in.

## 8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the
Service, then for any period required to comply with our legal obligations, resolve
disputes, and enforce our agreements. In particular:

- **AI request logs** are deleted automatically after a short period (currently about seven
  days).
- **Requests to specialists** expire approximately 30 days after they are sent to a business. A specialist's request to be introduced lapses after 14 days unanswered.
- **A deleted item** is kept for 30 days. During that time it is listed only in **Settings → Recently deleted**, to you and to anyone who could have deleted it, where it can be restored, and where its owner can delete it for good sooner; it is left out of your lists, totals, documents and downloads. After 30 days it is erased, with its photos and documents.
- **Backups:** our database provider keeps daily backups of the database on its own
  schedule (currently about a week), and we keep encrypted nightly backups of the database
  and of stored files, including photos and documents, for 30 days. Information you
  delete, including a deleted account, can remain in these backups until they expire.
- When you delete your account, we begin deletion after a **30-day grace period** during
  which you can cancel (see Section 9).

Backups and copies already delivered to independent third parties (for example, a specialist business that received an item snapshot, or someone who accepted a record from you) may persist under those parties' own retention practices. Our standard schedule: account data is retained while your account is active and is deleted within 30 days after a verified deletion request completes; operational AI-processing logs are retained for approximately 7 days; encrypted backups are kept as described above, for no more than 30 days.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. **We honour
these rights for all users where feasible, regardless of location.**

- **Access / know** (GDPR Art. 15; CCPA): request a copy of the personal information we
  hold about you and details about how we use it.
- **Portability** (GDPR Art. 20): receive certain data in a portable format. You can export your data at any time. **Settings → Download everything** produces a machine-readable file of your account's data — your profile, items, collections and who you share them with, beneficiaries, bequests, requests to specialists, and activity log — with a spreadsheet of your records and an archive of your photos. It leaves out sign-in secrets (such as the hashed recovery codes), our usage counters, your block list and the reports you filed, and any item in Recently deleted. To ask for something it leaves out, email us.
- **Correction / rectification** (GDPR Art. 16): correct inaccurate information, most of
  which you can edit directly in the App.
- **Deletion / erasure** ("right to be forgotten," GDPR Art. 17; CCPA): request deletion of your account and associated data from **Settings → Delete account**, or, without the App, as described at web.heirloomapp.io/delete-account. Deletion is subject to a **30-day grace period**, during which you may cancel the request; after that we delete your account and associated personal data, including your photographs and uploaded documents. Deletion runs as a background job; the screen shows its progress and tells you if it does not finish. We keep only what we are required or permitted to retain by law, and one thing you have already given away: a record someone has already accepted from you, which is theirs and stays with them, including the name you had when you sent it.
- **Restriction and objection** (GDPR Arts. 18, 21): ask us to limit or stop certain
  processing, including profiling; and object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent:** where processing is based on consent (including device permissions
  for camera, photos, and notifications, and your permission for AI features), you can
  withdraw it, for example through your device settings or **Settings → Advisor
  consent**, without affecting prior processing.
- **Non-discrimination** (CCPA): we will not discriminate against you for exercising your
  rights.
- **Opt out of "sale"/"sharing"** (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal
  information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of; if this
  changes we will provide a mechanism.

To exercise any right that is not available in-app, email **hello@heirloomapp.io**. We may need
to verify your identity before acting. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with
your local data-protection authority.

**Additional state disclosures (US):** we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under applicable US state privacy laws, and we do not process sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit — for California,
Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut, and similar laws, you may designate an authorized agent
and, where applicable, appeal a decision on your request.

## 10. Security

We use administrative and technical measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, authentication with optional two-factor sign-in, private storage with time-limited signed links for item photos and documents, and row-level access controls that scope your data to your account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Heirloom's operators can see your records when they need to, for support and for moderation.

Profile photos (avatars) are served from publicly readable URLs, so anyone who obtains the link can view yours. Everything else you upload — item photos and documents — is kept in private storage and shown through time-limited signed links.

### 12.1 If your business is listed in our directory

Heirloom's directory lists specialist businesses from public sources (Section 3.3), so that someone with an item to ask about can find one. If your business is listed:

- **What the listing shows.** Your business's name, type, city and state, specialties, website and association memberships, and whether the listing has been claimed. It shows no email address, phone number, street address or rating.
- **What else we hold.** Where the public source gave them: a contact email address, a phone number and a street address, and, for a listing found through a mapping service, the identifier it has there. We keep no rating, rating count, map location or category from the mapping service.
- **Emails from us.** At launch we email only a business that has claimed its listing. If we turn on requests to a business that has not, and someone asks yours about an item, we will email the contact address we hold: that an owner has a question, the kind of help asked for and the item's broad category, with links to read the request, to claim the listing, or to stop these emails. A business that has not claimed its listing gets at most one such email every 30 days; requests that arrive in between are listed together in the next one.
- **Claiming it.** Write to support@heirloomapp.io from an address at the business, or, once those emails are on, use "Claim your listing" in one, which lets you make an account, or sign in, and take the listing over: you can then answer requests in your own account, and each new request is emailed to you in full as it is sent.
- **Removing it.** "Stop these emails" or "Remove my business from Heirloom" in an email from us takes the listing out of the directory and stops the emails. If you ask us to remove the listing ("Is this your business? Claim or remove it" on the listing, or an email to support@heirloomapp.io), we also delete its email address, phone number, street address, website and what the mapping service gave, and keep only its name, its city and state, and a mark that it was removed, so that it is not listed or emailed again. Its contact details also come out of the notes we added to owners' requests; what the business itself wrote to an owner in reply to a request, its contact details included, stays with that owner's request.
