Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

The Peninsular Company, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Detroit ("Heirloom," "we," "us," or "our"), operates the Heirloom mobile and web application (the "App" or "Service"), which helps you keep an inventory of what is in your home: what each item is, its photos and its story, the documents and evidence behind its value, and who should inherit it. From that inventory it can prepare a document for your insurer or your family. Where you choose, it uses artificial intelligence to help identify and research your items, and, for accounts in the United States, it can connect you with independent specialist businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

By using Heirloom, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Heirloom is provided by The Peninsular Company, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Detroit. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@heirloomapp.io.

Heirloom is offered in the United States, to people who live there. Heirloom's iPhone App is offered on the App Store in the United States. The web App can be reached from other countries, and a relative abroad can open a collection shared with them; an account outside the United States is not offered specialists, sale conversations, sold prices or AI-estimated value ranges (Section 3.1).

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our representative / Data Protection contact is hello@heirloomapp.io (we have not appointed a formal EEA/UK representative or Data Protection Officer).

2. Summary — the short version

  • We collect the information you give us (your account details, the country you live in, the photos, descriptions and documents of your items, and any estate-planning information you enter) and some technical information about your device.

Payments. We take no payment from people who keep an inventory in the App, and we collect no payment information. A specialist business that buys introduction credits is invoiced outside the App (Section 12.1).

4. How artificial intelligence features work

AI identification and research are optional and need your permission. The App asks before it sends anything to an AI or search provider, and nothing is sent until you choose Allow. You can record your items yourself instead, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Advisor consent. Once you allow it, to help identify, describe, and research your items, we send the relevant item photos and descriptive text to third-party AI providers. Today we use OpenAI for image analysis, for reading marks and labels in your photos, and for the in-app Advisor chat. For finding comparable sales, we submit a temporary link to the item's photo to a reverse-image search service (SerpAPI, which queries Google Lens) and text queries built from the item's details, such as maker, type of item, period, and material, to a market-research search service (SerpAPI, which queries Google Search). We may in the future use additional AI providers (for example Anthropic); if we do, they will receive the same categories of item data for the same purpose, and we will update this Policy.

Sold prices. To find sales of the same kind of item, we send the search service the maker, form and one distinguisher on the record (a pattern, model or shape number). Our server then reads the public sale pages that search finds, eBay's included, without signing in to any site, and sends those pages nothing of yours. It reads them under its own name, HeirloomBot, and does not read a page that the site's robots.txt asks it not to.

A question about everything you own. If you ask the Advisor about your whole inventory, we send OpenAI the name, type, maker and value of every record you own, and no photos.

Items you share. Something about an item goes to these providers only if the item's owner has allowed it. If someone you share a collection with asks the Advisor about one of your items, both of you must have allowed it, and a Viewer or a Commenter is answered only from what their share shows (Section 6).

Please note: AI-generated identifications and valuations are automated, uncertain estimates for your information only. They are not professional appraisals, authentications, or insurance valuations, and you should not rely on them for insurance, tax, legal, estate, or sale decisions without independent professional advice.

AI request logging. For quality, debugging, and abuse-prevention purposes, our backend logs each AI request. By default a log entry keeps what diagnosis needs — which feature ran, its timing, status, model, and cost, and your account and item identifiers — and not your descriptions, the AI's answers, or links to your photos. To investigate a specific problem we may briefly turn on more detailed logging, which also records the text sent to and returned by the AI provider and links to the photos involved. All AI request logs are deleted automatically after a short period (currently about seven days).

Where we rely on legal bases under the GDPR/UK GDPR, they are typically: performance of a contract with you (providing the Service); your consent (for example, the AI features and certain other optional features, and where required, analytics and device permissions such as camera, photos, and notifications); our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service, preventing abuse); and compliance with legal obligations.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, then for any period required to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. In particular:

  • AI request logs are deleted automatically after a short period (currently about seven days).
  • Requests to specialists expire approximately 30 days after they are sent to a business. A specialist's request to be introduced lapses after 14 days unanswered.
  • A deleted item is kept for 30 days. During that time it is listed only in Settings → Recently deleted, to you and to anyone who could have deleted it, where it can be restored, and where its owner can delete it for good sooner; it is left out of your lists, totals, documents and downloads. After 30 days it is erased, with its photos and documents.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps daily backups of the database on its own schedule (currently about a week), and we keep encrypted nightly backups of the database and of stored files, including photos and documents, for 30 days. Information you delete, including a deleted account, can remain in these backups until they expire.
  • When you delete your account, we begin deletion after a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel (see Section 9).

Backups and copies already delivered to independent third parties (for example, a specialist business that received an item snapshot, or someone who accepted a record from you) may persist under those parties' own retention practices. Our standard schedule: account data is retained while your account is active and is deleted within 30 days after a verified deletion request completes; operational AI-processing logs are retained for approximately 7 days; encrypted backups are kept as described above, for no more than 30 days.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. We honour these rights for all users where feasible, regardless of location.

  • Access / know (GDPR Art. 15; CCPA): request a copy of the personal information we hold about you and details about how we use it.
  • Portability (GDPR Art. 20): receive certain data in a portable format. You can export your data at any time. Settings → Download everything produces a machine-readable file of your account's data — your profile, items, collections and who you share them with, beneficiaries, bequests, requests to specialists, and activity log — with a spreadsheet of your records and an archive of your photos. It leaves out sign-in secrets (such as the hashed recovery codes), our usage counters, your block list and the reports you filed, and any item in Recently deleted. To ask for something it leaves out, email us.
  • Correction / rectification (GDPR Art. 16): correct inaccurate information, most of which you can edit directly in the App.
  • Deletion / erasure ("right to be forgotten," GDPR Art. 17; CCPA): request deletion of your account and associated data from Settings → Delete account, or, without the App, as described at web.heirloomapp.io/delete-account. Deletion is subject to a 30-day grace period, during which you may cancel the request; after that we delete your account and associated personal data, including your photographs and uploaded documents. Deletion runs as a background job; the screen shows its progress and tells you if it does not finish. We keep only what we are required or permitted to retain by law, and one thing you have already given away: a record someone has already accepted from you, which is theirs and stays with them, including the name you had when you sent it.
  • Restriction and objection (GDPR Arts. 18, 21): ask us to limit or stop certain processing, including profiling; and object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent (including device permissions for camera, photos, and notifications, and your permission for AI features), you can withdraw it, for example through your device settings or Settings → Advisor consent, without affecting prior processing.
  • Non-discrimination (CCPA): we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Opt out of "sale"/"sharing" (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of; if this changes we will provide a mechanism.

To exercise any right that is not available in-app, email hello@heirloomapp.io. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Additional state disclosures (US): we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under applicable US state privacy laws, and we do not process sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit — for California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut, and similar laws, you may designate an authorized agent and, where applicable, appeal a decision on your request.

10. Security

We use administrative and technical measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, authentication with optional two-factor sign-in, private storage with time-limited signed links for item photos and documents, and row-level access controls that scope your data to your account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Heirloom's operators can see your records when they need to, for support and for moderation.

Profile photos (avatars) are served from publicly readable URLs, so anyone who obtains the link can view yours. Everything else you upload — item photos and documents — is kept in private storage and shown through time-limited signed links.

11. Cookies, and what is kept on your device

On the web, we use essential storage (such as local storage) to keep you signed in and to operate the Service. We use TelemetryDeck for privacy-focused analytics as described above. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

On your device. So that your lists open quickly, the App keeps a copy of the item lists your account has already loaded (your own items, and the collections you own or can edit): on a phone, in the App's cache, which is not part of the phone's backup; on the web, in your browser's storage. The copy is sent nowhere. It is removed from a device when you sign out there, when a different account signs in there, and when you ask there for your account to be deleted. A device you do not open again keeps its copy until its system clears the cache or you remove the App. The App also keeps on the device your sign-in session, your recent searches, a record you have started and not yet saved, and, if you came by invitation, a note that you did.

12.1 If your business is listed in our directory

Heirloom's directory lists specialist businesses from public sources (Section 3.3), so that someone with an item to ask about can find one. If your business is listed:

  • What the listing shows. Your business's name, type, city and state, specialties, website and association memberships, and whether the listing has been claimed. It shows no email address, phone number, street address or rating.
  • What else we hold. Where the public source gave them: a contact email address, a phone number and a street address, and, for a listing found through a mapping service, the identifier it has there. We keep no rating, rating count, map location or category from the mapping service.
  • Emails from us. At launch we email only a business that has claimed its listing. If we turn on requests to a business that has not, and someone asks yours about an item, we will email the contact address we hold: that an owner has a question, the kind of help asked for and the item's broad category, with links to read the request, to claim the listing, or to stop these emails. A business that has not claimed its listing gets at most one such email every 30 days; requests that arrive in between are listed together in the next one.
  • Claiming it. Write to support@heirloomapp.io from an address at the business, or, once those emails are on, use "Claim your listing" in one, which lets you make an account, or sign in, and take the listing over: you can then answer requests in your own account, and each new request is emailed to you in full as it is sent.
  • Removing it. "Stop these emails" or "Remove my business from Heirloom" in an email from us takes the listing out of the directory and stops the emails. If you ask us to remove the listing ("Is this your business? Claim or remove it" on the listing, or an email to support@heirloomapp.io), we also delete its email address, phone number, street address, website and what the mapping service gave, and keep only its name, its city and state, and a mark that it was removed, so that it is not listed or emailed again. Its contact details also come out of the notes we added to owners' requests; what the business itself wrote to an owner in reply to a request, its contact details included, stays with that owner's request. When we load business lists again, a business with that name in that city is left out, and one with a name like it in the same state is held back until we have checked that it is a different business. We answer a request to remove a listing within 10 business days.
  • Credits. If your business buys introduction credits, we keep the packs it bought (how many credits, the price, the dates and the invoice reference) and which introductions used one. Payment is by invoice, outside the App, and we hold no card details.
