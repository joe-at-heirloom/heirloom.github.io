1. What Heirloom does

Heirloom helps you keep an inventory of what is in your home — what each item is, its photos and its story, the documents and evidence behind its value, and who should inherit it — and prepare from it a document for your insurer or your family. If you allow it, the Service uses artificial intelligence and third-party image- and web-search tools to generate estimates and research, which can be uncertain or wrong; you can also record everything yourself. For accounts in the United States, the Service has a directory of independent specialist businesses (such as appraisers, dealers, galleries, and auction houses), can connect you with them, and can let specialists know that you would consider selling an item. Heirloom is a software tool and an introduction platform. Heirloom is not a marketplace, appraiser, auctioneer, dealer, insurer, broker, fiduciary, or law firm; it does not list items for sale, take offers, or handle payments for any sale; and it does not provide professional appraisal, authentication, financial, insurance, tax, legal, or estate advice.
