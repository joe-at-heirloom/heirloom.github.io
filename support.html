Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the You tab and tap Settings (the gear beside the title). Under Privacy & data, tap Delete account, then Delete my account, and confirm. It works the same way when you sign in at web.heirloomapp.io.

The deletion is scheduled for 30 days later, and until then you can cancel it on the same screen with Cancel deletion. After that, your records and files are removed, and the screen shows the deletion while it runs. A record someone has already accepted from you stays with them. To keep a copy first, tap Download my data first on the same screen.

No longer have the app, or can’t sign in? web.heirloomapp.io/delete-account explains what is deleted and what is kept, and how to ask by email from the address you sign in with. The sign-in screen links to it too, as Delete an account.

How do I turn on two-factor sign-in?

Go to Settings → Sign-in & security. In the two-factor section, tap Turn on two-factor, scan the QR code with the app you use for sign-in codes (or tap Can’t scan? to see a setup key you can type in instead), and enter the 6-digit code that app shows. From then on, signing in with your password, Apple or Google also asks for a current code from it.

To turn it off, tap Turn off two-factor on the same screen, then Turn off, and enter a current code if the app asks for one.

When you turn it on, Heirloom shows ten recovery codes, once. Save them somewhere safe, away from your phone: Heirloom keeps no copy and cannot show them again. If you lose the app you use for sign-in codes, tap Use a recovery code when you sign in. Each recovery code gets you in once, and using one turns two-factor off, so you set it up again. Sign-in & security shows how many codes are left, and Make new recovery codes replaces them.

If you have lost your recovery codes too, tap Lost your recovery codes too? when you sign in. Heirloom can reset two-factor only for an account that already signed in with Apple when two-factor was turned on: you sign in with Apple, confirm a code sent to the account’s email address, and a person at Heirloom approves the reset. For any other account, the recovery codes are the only way back in.

I forgot my password, or want to change my sign-in email

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email and tap Send reset link. If you signed up with Apple or Google, keep using that button to sign in. The app can’t change the email address you sign in with yet: write to us from that address and we’ll help.

The Advisor and your data

What does the Advisor send, and to whom?

Nothing until you allow it. Heirloom asks before the Advisor sends a photo or a note for the first time, and nothing is sent until you choose Allow. Choose Record everything myself, or Not now — I’ll record it myself when the question comes up later, and you can keep every record yourself.

Once you allow it, Heirloom’s server sends:

  • To OpenAI: your photos, the notes and answers you add, and the details on the record, so the Advisor can suggest a name, describe the item, read marks and labels, ask useful questions and answer yours.
  • To SerpAPI, which searches Google Lens: a temporary link to a photo of the item, to look for similar items and comparable sales online.
  • To SerpAPI, which searches Google: the maker, type of item, material and period on the record, only when the maker is known, to look for auction records of similar items.
  • To SerpAPI, for the sold-price search: the maker, form, period and one distinguisher on the record (a pattern, model or shape number), to find sales of the same kind of item. Heirloom’s server then reads the public sold pages it finds, eBay’s included, and sends them nothing of yours.
  • To OpenAI, when you ask about everything you own: the name, type, maker and value of every record you own, and no photos.

Once you have allowed it, the Advisor’s first look and its search for similar items start on their own when you photograph an item. Each service handles what it receives under its own terms; the Privacy Policy has the details. To change your answer, go to Settings → Advisor consent. Choose Turn off the Advisor’s outside services and nothing new is sent; what is already on your records stays. If it is off, Review and allow asks the question again.

Why doesn’t my record show an estimate?

The Advisor offers an estimate only when it has found a recorded sale of a comparable item, and comparable sales can’t always be found. Asking prices and pre-sale estimates it finds are shown with what kind of price they are, and never set a figure on their own. A figure you enter yourself comes first, and the Advisor never replaces it. Many items have no market value, and a record is complete without one. Specialists, sale conversations, sold prices and Heirloom’s price estimates are offered in the United States only for now.

Does Heirloom tell me whether something is genuine?

No. The Advisor says what the photos show, what you told it, what a source supports and what is still unknown. Whether an item is genuine is a question for a specialist you choose.

Your records

How do I export my records?

  • Download everything: Settings → Download everything (on the You tab, Family & records opens it too) has three files to keep outside Heirloom. Download my data gives you everything stored under your account as one .json file, with photos as links rather than copies. Download the spreadsheet gives one row per item in the collection you have open, as a CSV file. Download the archive gives a ZIP of your items, collections, family stories and every photo, in one file that works without Heirloom.
  • For an insurer or your family: Family & records also makes an Inventory and insurance report and a Family packet, and Past outputs keeps a record of each version you made and whether you sent it.

Safety

How do I report something or block someone?

Where someone else’s words appear in Heirloom (a comment on a shared record, a message from a specialist, or a specialist’s request to be introduced), there is a Report control. Choose a reason and send the report. We review reports, usually within a day, and the outcome appears in Settings → Safety and reports; we also tell you in Notifications.

To block a specialist, use Block this specialist on their page, or block them from their request. They are not told, and they can’t contact you through Heirloom. In a shared collection, the owner can remove a member and a member can leave, in Settings → Collections & sharing. Your blocked accounts are listed in Settings → Safety and reports, where you can unblock them.

What Heirloom does not do

Does Heirloom sell items, take offers or handle money for a sale?

No. Heirloom never sells items or takes offers, and nothing is put up for sale. It takes no part in any sale and handles no money for one. If you open a record’s Sale conversation and choose I’d consider selling this, specialists in that field may see a short card about it (what it is and roughly where you are, never your name, photos, any value or your address) and may ask to be introduced. Nothing more is shared unless you accept. You can Withdraw at any time, which stops new specialists seeing it, though a specialist who already saw the card can’t unsee it.

When you ask a specialist for help yourself, you see exactly what will be shared before it goes. Any conversation after an introduction, and any sale, is between you and the specialist.
